# Deleting your data

You can delete the data BrandPilot holds about you in any of these ways.

## Disconnect a social account

In BrandPilot, open **Social Accounts** and choose **Disconnect** on the account. We delete its access token, its account details and its account metrics straight away. Posts you created in BrandPilot stay in your brand, no longer linked to that account.

## Remove BrandPilot from Facebook or Instagram

In Facebook, go to **Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites**, find BrandPilot and choose **Remove**. In Instagram, use **Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites**. Meta then tells us:

- the accounts you connected through that login stop working immediately, and their tokens are destroyed;
- if you also ask Meta to delete your data, we delete those connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, their metrics, and the platform post IDs and links of posts published through them, and give you a confirmation code.

Check the progress of a Meta deletion request with your confirmation code at [{{appUrl}}/data-deletion/status]({{appUrl}}/data-deletion/status).

## Delete your account or organisation

Email {{contact}} from your account's email address and say what you want deleted: your user account, a brand, or your whole organisation. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days. [Self-service deletion in Settings is being added.]

Records we must keep by law (for example billing records) and short-lived backups, until they expire. [Periods to be confirmed.]
