# Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data {{company}} ("we") processes when you use BrandPilot at {{appUrl}}, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Contact: {{contact}}.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** your name, email address and a hash of your password (we never store the password itself), and the organisations, brands and roles you belong to.
- **Brand content you provide:** brand descriptions, websites you ask us to analyse, uploaded logos, photos and documents, strategies, posts, captions and creatives.
- **Connected social accounts:** when you connect Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube or TikTok, the platform gives us an access token and basic account details (account ID, name, handle, profile picture). For Meta logins we also store your app-scoped Meta user ID, so we can act on Meta's data-deletion and deauthorisation requests. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
- **Platform metrics:** reach, impressions, likes, comments, shares and follower counts for posts published through BrandPilot and for connected accounts.
- **Usage and security data:** sign-ins, security-relevant actions (the Security log), AI usage records (which feature was used, token counts and estimated cost, never your prompts' content in those records), and a keyed hash of IP addresses for security events.

## How we use it

- To provide the service: analyse your brand, generate strategies and content, render creatives, and publish approved posts to the accounts you connected.
- To keep the service secure and reliable: authentication, rate limiting, abuse prevention, the Security log and error diagnostics.
- To bill and enforce plan limits.

Nothing is published without approval by a person in your organisation.
